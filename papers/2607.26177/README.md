# arXiv:2607.26177

## How to Raise a Supermassive Black Hole: Interpreting Early JWST AGN with the AESOPICA Simulations

Original paper:

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26177

## About this notebook

This notebook demonstrates the main ideas presented in the paper using a simplified educational implementation.

It is **not** a reproduction of the authors' complete pipeline. Instead, the notebook focuses on illustrating how efficient, self-regulated growth can erase the memory of a black hole's initial seed mass in a form that is easy to understand and experiment with.

Synthetic growth histories are used to keep the notebook self-contained and easy to run.

## Contents

- Eddington-limited black hole growth timescale;
- exponential and self-regulated growth models;
- comparison of four initial seed masses;
- visualization of convergence toward a common equilibrium mass.
