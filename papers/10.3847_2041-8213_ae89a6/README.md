## How Many Transiting Giant Planets Can JWST Search for Moons and Rotational Oblateness?

Original paper:

https://doi.org/10.3847/2041-8213/ae89a6

## About this notebook

This notebook demonstrates the main ideas presented in the paper using a simplified educational implementation.

It is **not** a reproduction of the authors' complete pipeline. Instead, the notebook focuses on illustrating the transit signals produced by rotational oblateness and a moon in a form that is easy to understand and experiment with.

Synthetic transit curves are generated from a geometric overlap model to keep the notebook self-contained and easy to run.

## Contents

- geometric transit model for a spherical and an oblate planet;
- comparison of the corresponding light curves and residual signal;
- simplified moon transit signal;
- visualizations of the expected signal amplitudes.
