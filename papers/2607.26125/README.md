# arXiv:2607.26125

## Emulating the Complex Galactic-Scale Orbital Dynamics of LISA Massive Black Hole Pairs with Normalizing Flows

Original paper:

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.26125

## About this notebook

This notebook demonstrates the main ideas presented in the paper using a simplified educational implementation.

It is **not** a reproduction of the authors' complete pipeline. Instead, the notebook focuses on illustrating how a rotating stellar bar can alter the orbital decay of a massive black hole in a form that is easy to understand and experiment with.

A semi-analytic toy model is used to keep the notebook self-contained and easy to run.

## Contents

- softened-needle potential for a stellar bar;
- Chandrasekhar dynamical-friction model;
- comparison of orbital decay with and without the bar;
- static plots and an animated orbit visualization.
