## About this notebook

This notebook demonstrates the main ideas presented in the paper using a simplified educational implementation.

It is not a reproduction of the authors’ complete analysis pipeline. Instead, the notebook focuses on illustrating how the properties of luminous quasars at z ∼ 5–6 can be studied through their emission lines, how black hole masses and Eddington ratios can be estimated, and how extended [O III] emission can reveal gas outflows, turbulent interstellar media, and possible galaxy mergers around rapidly growing supermassive black holes in the early Universe.
